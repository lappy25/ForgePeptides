📋 About Peptides

What are peptides? ▶

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that are the building blocks of proteins. They play crucial roles in muscle growth, fat loss, recovery, and overall cellular function. Our research peptides are designed for scientific study and personal research purposes.

Are your peptides of high quality? ▶

Yes! All our peptides are sourced from reputable suppliers and are manufactured under strict quality control standards. We test each batch for purity and potency to ensure the highest quality for our customers.

What's the difference between your peptides? ▶

Each peptide has unique benefits and mechanisms of action. For example, BPC-157 supports tissue healing, Sermorelin promotes natural growth hormone release, and TB-500 aids in injury recovery. Visit our Research Peptides page for detailed information on each one.

What are peptide stacks? ▶

Peptide stacks are carefully curated combinations of complementary peptides designed to work synergistically. These pre-made stacks help you achieve specific goals like fat loss, muscle gain, or overall wellness more effectively than single peptides.

🧪 Product Information

How should I store my peptides? ▶

Store peptides in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Most peptides should be refrigerated (2-8°C) after reconstitution. Keep them in airtight containers and away from moisture. Properly stored peptides remain potent for up to 24 months.

What is the shelf life of peptides? ▶

Unopened peptides have a shelf life of 24-36 months when stored properly. Once reconstituted, they should be used within 14-30 days depending on the specific peptide. Always check the expiration date on your package.

How is peptide purity verified? ▶

Every batch carries a third-party Certificate of Analysis (CoA) measuring purity via HPLC, mass spectrometry, and sterility tests. Purity thresholds are 98%+ for standard peptides and 99%+ for premium compounds. You may request the full report for your batch.

How do I know the peptides are genuine? ▶

All our peptides come with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from third-party labs. Each batch is tested for purity and potency. We also provide a QR code for authentication. If you have any concerns, contact our support team with your batch number.

Can I combine different peptides? ▶

Yes! Many peptides work well together. However, certain combinations may be more synergistic. Our pre-made stacks are scientifically formulated for optimal results. For custom combinations, consult our team for personalized recommendations.
